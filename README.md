AgriHelp is a user-friendly farming assistance website designed specifically for beginner farmers and small-scale farmers in Tamil Nadu with land holdings of less than one acre. The website helps farmers make informed decisions by providing personalized crop recommendations based on their location and farming conditions.
Users can also explore detailed crop guides that explain the complete cultivation process, including planting, watering, fertilization, crop care, and harvesting.
The platform also includes an AI-powered farming assistant that allows users to ask questions and receive simple, practical farming guidance. In addition, the community section enables farmers to share their experiences, post updates, ask questions, and learn from other farmers. With its simple, mobile-friendly interface and easy-to-understand information, AgriHelp aims to make farming knowledge more accessible to beginners and support them throughout their farming journey.
